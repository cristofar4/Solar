    Where should I start?

    Send the form with your last few electricity bills to hand. We’ll book a site assessment and you’ll have a fixed quote within a week.

    Can I upgrade my current system?

    Usually, yes. Most existing panels can take a battery, and many inverters can take more panels. We check yours on the first visit.

    What happens in a power cut?

    With a battery, your home switches over in milliseconds and keeps running. Without one, grid-tied panels shut down for the safety of line workers.
