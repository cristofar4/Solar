How it works

From first call to first kilowatt.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Day 1 · a 90-minute visit

    A surveyor checks your roof, wiring and meter, and reads a year of your bills so the plan starts from how you really use power.

  2. 02

    Design

    Week 1 · a fixed quote

Where should I start?

Send the form with a recent electricity bill to hand. We’ll book a site assessment and you’ll have a fixed quote within a week.

Can I add a battery to panels I already have?

Usually, yes. Most existing systems can take a battery, and many inverters can take more panels. We check yours on the first visit.

What happens in a power cut?

With a battery, your home switches over in milliseconds and keeps running. Without one, grid-tied panels shut down for the safety of line workers.

How long do panels last?

Panels are warrantied for 25 years and usually keep producing well beyond that, at a little less output each year.
